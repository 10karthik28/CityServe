# Kerala CityServe - User Guide

## Introduction
Welcome to **Kerala CityServe**! This is your official digital portal for reporting, tracking, and resolving municipal issues right from your neighborhood in Ernakulam. Whether it's a persistent water leak, an uncollected waste pile, or a broken street light, CityServe connects you directly to the correct department authority to get it fixed quickly.

---

## 1. Getting Started
If you are new to the platform, you will need to create a Citizen account.
1. Click on the **Register** link on the home page.
2. Enter your Name, Email, Phone Number, and a secure password.
3. Once registered, use your email and password to **Login** to your dashboard.

---

## 2. How to Report a Problem
Found a neighborhood issue? Here is how to alert the authorities:
1. Navigate to the **Request Service** page from the top navigation bar.
2. **Select the Service Type:** Choose the category that best fits your problem (e.g., "Water Supply" or "Street Lighting"). The system will automatically route your complaint to the respective agency (like the Kerala Water Authority or KSEB).
3. **Set the Location:**
   - You can type your location (e.g., "Kakkanad") in the search box to find it.
   - Alternatively, you can click directly on the interactive map to place a pin exactly where the issue is.
4. **Provide Details:** Add a clear description of the problem.
5. **Attach a Photo (Optional):** You can upload an image of the problem directly from your device which can greatly help field personnel understand the severity.
6. Click **Submit Request**. You will be given a unique Request ID (e.g., `REQ-3829`).

---

## 3. Tracking Your Request
You don't need to make phone calls to figure out what's happening.
- Go to the **Check Status** page.
- Here, you'll see a complete list of your reported issues.
- You can watch the status update in real-time as it moves from *"Requested to the authority"* directly to *"Work done"*.
- You can also see the exact name and phone number of the field worker assigned to solve your problem!

---

## 4. Giving Feedback
Your voice matters. 
Once your issue is marked as fully resolved ("Work done"), a Rate button will appear on the issue card.
- You can give the service a 1 to 5 Star Rating based on how quickly and effectively the problem was solved.
- You can also leave written comments for the authority to review.

---

## 5. For Municipal Authorities
If you are a department worker or admin (e.g., KSEB Admin):
- Ensure you select **"Authority Login"** on the login page.
- Your dashboard is customized to only show reports relevant to your department.
- You will have access to powerful tools allowing you to assign field workers, update project statuses, and view automated feedback analytical reports.
